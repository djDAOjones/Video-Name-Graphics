# Video Name Graphics

A browser-only app that makes a name graphic — a lower third with a name
and an optional second line — from a short form, and saves it as a
transparent video to lay over other video in Clipchamp, or as a still
PNG. Nothing typed leaves the device.

It is the sibling of the
[UoN Video Finisher](https://github.com/djDAOjones/UoN-Video-Finisher),
and keeps its simple, accessible form and look. Unlike the Finisher it
is built to travel: brands are packs chosen at build time (see
[brands/README.md](brands/README.md)), and the default build carries no
University branding.
